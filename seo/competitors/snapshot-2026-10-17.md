## Differentiation opportunities for CodeXchange
- French-first + tech jobs + projects + mentorship in one place (most competitors are single-focus).
- Developer tools (code snippets, salary guide) — none offer free tools.
- Local events + remote jobs combined.
- Verification badge + gamified karma (niveau) — not common.
