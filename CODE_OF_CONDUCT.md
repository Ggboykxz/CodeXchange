# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment‑free experience for everyone, regardless of age, gender identity and expression, sexual orientation, disability, physical appearance, body size, race, ethnicity, religion (or lack thereof), or technology choices.

## Expected Behavior
- Be respectful and considerate.
- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Be mindful of tone and jokes; avoid sarcasm that could be misinterpreted.
- Respect personal boundaries and privacy.
- Share constructive feedback; be open to receiving it.

## Unacceptable Behavior
- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination of any kind.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or hateful language.
- Unsolicited sexual attention or harassment.
- Publishing private information about other contributors.

## Reporting
If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please contact the project maintainers at **security@code-x-change.dev** (or open a private issue). All reports will be handled promptly and confidentially.

## Enforcement
Maintainers will take appropriate action, up to and including banning from the project.
